# Business Viability Update — Phase-2 Simulation Layer

## Value Unlock

Phase-2 converts the digital twin from passive analytics into **operational rehearsal infrastructure**, unlocking:

- Scenario planning workshops with district collectors.
- Investor-grade storytelling (“watch water climb your actual terrain”).
- Procurement differentiation vs static GIS dashboards.

## Monetization Levers

1. **Simulation minutes** — metered GPU/CPU bursts for large ensembles.
2. **Hotspot packs** — premium add-ons for cyclone corridors (Puri, Kendrapara, Jagatsinghpur baseline already wired).
3. **API bundles** — NDRF / SDRF integrations for automated situation reports.
4. **Training & certification** — EOC operator licensing tied to simulation sandboxes.

## Risk Mitigation

- Position system as **decision support**, not regulatory hydrology sign-off.
- Pair with subject matter experts for calibration workshops (Phase-3).
- Bundle indemnity language for meteorological uncertainty.

## Go-To-Market Angle

Use coastal Odisha rehearsal clips in grants, CSR tenders, and resilience accelerators—demos now include live controls (rainfall, velocity, timeline).
